# 相機測試素材說明

`RP5相機測試.html` 是離線測試頁，可顯示對準、色彩、形狀、移動、文字及特徵測試畫面。「AI 生成物件圖」需要同一目錄中的 `AI生成物件測試.png`。

`AI生成物件測試.png` 是使用內建 ImageGen 圖片生成工具製作的合成素材，不是真實拍攝的物件照片。第 40 課再用 RP5 相機拍攝這張顯示在螢幕上的素材；相機照片與模型結果都已在該課標明。

生成提示詞：

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create a photorealistic teaching test image for a university computer vision lesson: a single red apple, a yellow banana, and a white ceramic mug with a handle, all separated and fully visible, resting on a plain light gray tabletop, photographed straight-on in soft even daylight with an uncluttered background. Realistic ordinary objects, clear silhouettes, no people, hands, logos, labels, text, or watermarks. Landscape composition designed for display on a computer monitor and later photographed by a Raspberry Pi camera.
