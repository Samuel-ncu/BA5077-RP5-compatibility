# 相機測試素材說明

學生可先閱讀[圖片、程式與測試素材下載及使用方式](./素材下載與使用.md)。

`RP5相機測試.html` 是離線測試頁，可顯示對準、色彩、形狀、移動、文字及特徵測試畫面。「AI 生成物件圖」需要同一目錄中的 `AI生成物件測試.png`。

`AI生成物件測試.png` 是使用內建 ImageGen 圖片生成工具製作的合成素材，不是真實拍攝的物件照片。第 40 課再用 RP5 相機拍攝這張顯示在螢幕上的素材；相機照片與模型結果都已在該課標明。

生成提示詞：

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create a photorealistic teaching test image for a university computer vision lesson: a single red apple, a yellow banana, and a white ceramic mug with a handle, all separated and fully visible, resting on a plain light gray tabletop, photographed straight-on in soft even daylight with an uncluttered background. Realistic ordinary objects, clear silhouettes, no people, hands, logos, labels, text, or watermarks. Landscape composition designed for display on a computer monitor and later photographed by a Raspberry Pi camera.

`lesson28-moving-target.avi` 是用程式繪製的**生成動畫**，不是相機實拍，也不是 Thonny 的驗證成果：淺灰底、黑框、上方英文標題 `MOVING RED TARGET`，一個紅色圓形左右來回移動。影片為 960×540、24 FPS、720 張影格（約 30 秒），編碼 MJPG，約 14 MB。用途：沒有相機或還沒架好螢幕時，可用 `cv2.VideoCapture('lesson28-moving-target.avi')` 讀取，練習第 28 課的紅色 HSV 遮罩與追蹤；也可以在電腦螢幕全螢幕播放，代替測試頁讓相機拍攝。第 28 課主線使用的是測試頁「移動追蹤」，課文中的實測數字都不是用這支影片得到的。

`AI生成臉與手.png` 使用內建 ImageGen 產生，是虛構人物，不是真人照片。本課先在螢幕顯示素材，再由 RP5 拍攝；完整步驟參考[相機實拍與校正流程](./相機實拍與校正流程.md)。生成提示詞：

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create a single photorealistic classroom test image of an entirely fictional adult person, front-facing head and upper body with a neutral friendly expression, looking at the camera, holding one open hand beside the face with palm facing the camera and all five fingers clearly separated and fully visible. Plain light background, evenly lit, no other people, objects, logos, text or watermark. Frame both the complete face and entire hand large enough for facial landmark and hand landmark computer vision exercises. The image will be shown on a computer screen and photographed by a Raspberry Pi camera; this is an AI-generated test fixture, not a real person.
